# KITRO

> KITRO is a AI-powered food waste management for commercial and professional kitchens, across hotels, resorts, hospitals, cruises, schools & universities, and canteens.

## 🇬🇧 English

### About

KITRO is a Swiss company founded in 2017 that helps professional kitchens reduce food waste through AI-powered automation. We combine IoT devices, computer vision, and data analytics to make food waste tracking seamless and actionable.


### What We Do

KITRO provides an automated food waste tracking solution for hospitality and food service businesses worldwide. Our system uses:

- **KITRO TARE**: An AI-powered scale and camera device that automatically identifies and weighs food waste
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Real-time insights and actionable recommendations
- **Expert Consulting**: Personalized support to help kitchens optimize operations

### Key Benefits

- Reduce food waste by up to 60%
- Increase food profit margins by 8%
- Lower carbon footprint
- No workflow disruption - plug and play installation
- Automated data collection with AI recognition

### Industries We Serve

Hotels and hospitality, hospitals and healthcare, universities and schools, corporate catering and canteens, restaurants and cruise ships

### Results

Our clients typically achieve: up to 60% reduction in food waste, significant CO₂ reduction, compliance with growing EU food waste regulations
